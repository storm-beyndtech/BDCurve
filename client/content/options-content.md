## Risk Disclosures

### Important Information

**Options Are Leveraged Instruments:**
Small investments can lead to large gains **or losses**. You can lose more than your initial investment, especially with naked strategies.

**Complexity:**
Options strategies require understanding of:

- Underlying asset movements
- Time decay effects
- Volatility changes
- Interest rate impacts
- Dividend implications

**Assignment Risk:**
Short options can be assigned at any time, potentially resulting in stock ownership (calls) or stock purchase obligations (puts).

**Expiration Risk:**
Options expire worthless if out-of-the-money at expiration, resulting in 100% loss of premium paid.

**Early Exercise:**
American-style options can be exercised early, especially before ex-dividend dates.

**Liquidity:**
Some options have wide bid-ask spreads or low volume, making entry/exit difficult.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I receive my rebates?**
A: Rebates are automatically credited to your account monthly based on your trading volume.

**Q: What options level do I need for rebates?**
A: All options levels receive rebates on eligible stock and ETF option contracts.

**Q: Do index options receive rebates?**
A: No, index options (SPX, NDX, VIX, etc.) are charged $0.35-$0.50 per contract.

**Q: Can I trade options in my IRA?**
A: Yes! IRA accounts can trade covered calls, cash-secured puts, and spreads (no naked options).

**Q: What is the margin requirement for options?**
A: Varies by strategy. Covered calls use stock as collateral. Cash-secured puts require 100% cash. Spreads require the max loss amount.

**Q: Can I exercise options early?**
A: Yes, American-style options can be exercised anytime before expiration.

**Q: What happens at expiration?**
A: ITM options are automatically exercised. OTM options expire worthless.
