# ETF Trading on Bounding Curve

## What is an ETF?

An **Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF)** is a collection of multiple stocks, bonds, or other assets bundled together as a single investment. ETFs provide instant diversification and trade on exchanges just like individual stocks.

**Key Benefits:**

- Diversification in one transaction
- Lower costs than mutual funds
- Real-time trading during market hours
- Tax efficiency
- Transparency in holdings

## ETF vs. Mutual Funds

### Key Differences

| Feature            | ETFs                          | Mutual Funds                  |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Trading**        | Real-time during market hours | Once daily after market close |
| **Pricing**        | Continuous intraday pricing   | End-of-day NAV                |
| **Commissions**    | $0 on Bounding Curve           | May have sales loads          |
| **Minimums**       | As low as $1 (fractional)     | Often $500-$3,000             |
| **Transparency**   | Holdings disclosed daily      | Disclosed quarterly           |
| **Tax Efficiency** | Generally more tax-efficient  | Less tax-efficient            |
| **Expense Ratios** | Typically lower               | Typically higher              |

## ETF Selection Guide

### What to Consider

**Investment Objectives**

- Growth vs. income
- Time horizon
- Risk tolerance
- Tax situation

**Fund Characteristics**

- **Expense ratio** - Lower is generally better
- **AUM** - Larger funds typically more liquid
- **Trading volume** - Higher volume = tighter spreads
- **Tracking error** - How closely it follows the index
- **Holdings** - Understand what you own

**Performance Metrics**

- Historical returns (1, 5, 10 years)
- Volatility and maximum drawdown
- Sharpe ratio (risk-adjusted returns)
- Yield and distribution history

## Risk Considerations

**ETF-Specific Risks:**

- Market risk - Value fluctuates with underlying assets
- Liquidity risk - Some ETFs trade infrequently
- Tracking error - May not perfectly match index
- Premium/discount to NAV - May trade above/below asset value

**Asset-Specific Risks:**

- Stock ETFs - Market volatility
- Bond ETFs - Interest rate risk
- International ETFs - Currency and political risk
- Commodity ETFs - High volatility
- Leveraged ETFs - Amplified losses (not recommended for buy-and-hold)
