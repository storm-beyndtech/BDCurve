## Risk Disclosures

### Important Considerations

**High Volatility:**
Cryptocurrency prices can be extremely volatile, with rapid and substantial price movements in short periods.

**Market Manipulation:**
Crypto markets may be susceptible to manipulation, especially for lower-volume tokens.

**Regulatory Uncertainty:**
The regulatory landscape for cryptocurrency is evolving and may impact future operations.

**No Insurance:**
Cryptocurrencies are **not FDIC or SIPC insured**. While we carry crime insurance, crypto investments don't have the same protections as traditional securities.

**Technology Risks:**
Blockchain networks may experience technical issues, forks, or security vulnerabilities.

**Loss of Access:**
If you lose access to your account credentials or send crypto to wrong addresses, recovery may be impossible.

**Tax Implications:**
Cryptocurrency transactions may have tax consequences. Consult with a tax professional.
